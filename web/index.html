Not a doctor.
By design.

  • Siaga never diagnoses or prescribes a drug or dose.
  • It never invents symptoms, allergies or first-aid steps. Every step comes from a bundled, pre-approved guide.
  • Call 112 stays visible and comes before the draft message.
  • When severity is uncertain, Siaga raises it. You can correct the brief before acting.
  • Siaga treats your note as data. Text inside it can never override the safety rules.

Works in Airplane Mode.

Apple Foundation Models run directly on your iPhone. No network, no cloud, no backend, no login.

If Apple Intelligence is unavailable, Siaga switches to a keyword fallback and labels it on screen.

Runs on iPhones with Apple Intelligence.
